# Ndea

> Ndea is an AI research and science laboratory founded by Francois Chollet (creator of Keras and the ARC-AGI benchmark) and Mike Knoop (co-founder of Zapier) to build frontier AI systems that blend intuitive pattern recognition with formal reasoning into a unified architecture. Ndea pursues program synthesis, enhanced by deep learning, as a pathway to artificial general intelligence, with the goal of building "a factory for rapid scientific advancement." Ndea publishes no public API, developer portal, or SDK at this time; this profile tracks the company's public web surface.
